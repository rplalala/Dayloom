roles and quantity review. Current rules belong to [requirements](../../0002-requirements-evidence.md)
and [Repair](../../0005-validation-repair%28v3%29.md). Local paths identify private evidence;
these pilots were not controlled quality comparisons.

## Semantic and completion corrections

principal-role exceptions. Named repeat visits used their own count obligation rather
than a duplicate unsupported semantic goal. Review unified canonical counting across labels,
protected count/date goals and propagated named completion through Product JSON/SSE.

Bounded cases exercised eight distinct visits, exact-two museums on separate dates and
repeated British Museum visits. A rich-trip case first failed interpretation, then source
references, before a separate corrected sample returned 14 visits/four quantity gaps.
Repair was disabled in that sample; gaps were not evidence of exhausted rounds.

Default-on quantity review followed on 26 September, with overfull still separately
controlled and the obsolete optional repetition switch removed. The final policy gate
passed 1,648 backend tests with nine skips; Product propagation passed 59 frontend tests.
An earlier exported-tree run failed for missing local dependencies before a provisioned
selection passed; those subset counts were not combined into another full invocation.
